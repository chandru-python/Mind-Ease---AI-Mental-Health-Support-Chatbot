About the mental health chatbot

An empathetic AI companion built with LSTM, NLP, and mood tracking — designed for supportive conversation.

LSTM deep learning

Bidirectional LSTM layers (64 units) with attention-like pooling. Trained on emotional text data to capture context and sequence.

val accuracy ~88%

NLP pipeline

Tokenizer (10k words) + padding + TF‑IDF vectorizer for response retrieval. Cleaning: lower case, punctuation removal.

spaCy inspired

mood tracking

Every interaction logs emotion (joy, sadness, anger, etc.). Weekly/monthly trends visualized in your private dashboard.

privacy first

emotion detection

7 emotion classes – LSTM confidence + threshold

coping suggestions

context‑aware responses via cosine similarity

mood dashboard

track weekly patterns, export insights

To offer a private, intelligent space where people can express emotions and receive gentle, evidence‑informed support. The LSTM model captures sequential language nuances, while the mood dashboard helps users recognise patterns.
